## Stat: Global Textile Waste (context only, not trend-specific)

Ellen MacArthur Foundation. (2017). *A new textiles economy: Redesigning fashion's future.*
https://www.ellenmacarthurfoundation.org/news/one-garbage-truck-of-textiles-wasted-every-second-report-creates-vision-for-change

Figures cited: the equivalent of one garbage truck of textiles is landfilled or burned every second
globally; an estimated $500 billion in value is lost yearly to clothing that's barely worn and rarely
recycled. Report published 2017, note the year on the slide citation so it doesn't read as current
data. Used as general context for fast-fashion waste, not evidence about mob-wife purchases specifically.
