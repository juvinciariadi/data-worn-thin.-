# Content Pillars

Ratio guidance: pillars 1 through 4 make up the majority of posts. Pillar 5 is a lighter, occasional treat (roughly 1 in 4 to 5 posts), visually tagged as a distinct recurring series so followers learn to recognize its different register.

## 1. Price-Per-Wear vs. Real Wages
Hours of minimum-wage work needed to afford a fast-fashion haul, indexed against inflation.

## 2. Trend Cycle Speed
How fast micro-trends are manufactured to be discarded, and what that means for landfill and labor.

## 3. Who Actually Gets Paid
Markup breakdowns: garment worker wage vs. retail price vs. brand margin.

## 4. "Affordable" ≠ Cheap
Is fast fashion rational for low-income consumers, or a trap? Cost-of-living context, no judgment.

## 5. Trend Forecast, With Receipts *(the fun pillar)*
Still calls upcoming trends, but always adds a one-line "cost of that trend" twist so it stays on-brand rather than reading as a departure.
