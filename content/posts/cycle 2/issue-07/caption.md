We remember the revenge dress. One dress, one night, one legendary read on an ex.

We don't really remember the years of rewearing that came before and after it. The same skirt, restyled. The same jumper, worn again. That part never made for as good a story.

But the repetition was the actually radical part. Buying less and wearing it more isn't a personality trait, it's math. It just so happens that the math only works if you can afford to wait for it to pay off.

Some things really do come back around. Even a bag can end up on someone else's arm, decades later, still doing the same job it always did.
