## Slide 3 — THE STAT

**Label:** THE STAT.

**Headline:** Some things get *kept*.

**Body:**

A cheap top bought for one night out rarely survives the season. Lower quality, chasing a trend, worn once and done.

An expensive piece, though, tends to stick around, even unworn. Not because it fits perfectly. Because it feels wasteful to let it go.

**Citation (in-text):** Morgan & Birtwistle, 2009

## Slide 6 — THE MATH

**Label:** THE MATH.

**Headline:** Five years, two totals.

**Body:**

Trend-chasing adds up to **$2,898**. Buying one durable piece and replacing it only when it wears out adds up to **$486**.

The gap keeps growing every year.

## Slide 7 — THE CATCH

**Label:** THE CATCH.

**Headline:** The math works. The *upfront* doesn't.

**Body:**

A durable piece pays for itself over time. But "over time" isn't the problem. The problem is the price tag on day one.

Not everyone has that much cash sitting around for one jumper, even if it's the cheaper choice in the long run.
