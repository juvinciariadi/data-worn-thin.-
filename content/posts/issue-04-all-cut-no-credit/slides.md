`Slide 5 — THE BRIDGE`
 
**Headline:** So why does labor lose first?
Supporting line: Factories compete against each other for the order. Brands negotiate a price, then negotiate it down again. Materials and shipping are fixed costs nobody can shrink. 
Wages are the one line that *can* move, so it's the one that does.

Aside: "Less villain-boss drama, more a hundred small, completely legal decisions, each one squeezing the same narrow line a little thinner."
