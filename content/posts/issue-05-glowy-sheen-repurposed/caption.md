Every few years, the same promise gets a new name. Skin tint. Tinted moisturizer. Serum foundation. Skin-like finish. The industry isn't really selling something new, it's selling the feeling of catching up. And catching up isn't the same for everyone. Some people found what works and stopped looking. Others are still chasing the next name for the same thing, one bottle at a time.

Same glow. Only some of us keep buying it twice.
