The Denim Divide. 🧵

Same $21 jeans. Wildly different amount of time to earn them, depending on your wage.
The math says buy once, buy well. But “eventually cheaper” doesn’t help much if you can’t afford “eventually.”
No judgment, just the numbers, and a genuine curiosity about who gets to plan for 43 wears from now.
