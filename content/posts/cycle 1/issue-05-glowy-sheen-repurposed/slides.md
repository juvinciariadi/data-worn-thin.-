## `Slide 4 — THE FOCUS`
 
**Headline:** It's not a fluke. It's a formula.
Chart 1: Google Search Interest – four names, one promise (Google Trends, US)
**Supporting line:** While "skin tint" crashed, the underlying idea kept selling under other aliases, tinted moisturizer, BB cream, serum foundation, even "skin-like finish," a phrase that's on every product description right now that almost nobody actually searches for. 

It's not just makeup chasing this either, skincare brands are making the same claim from the other perspective, promising the glow without any tint at all.

**Headline:** $134.49 to chase currency, 
$7.50 to choose sustainability.

**Supporting line:** Girl math says it doesn't count if it's five separate purchases. It's still $134.49. All the above share the same core promise, with a huge price gap. A real share of what gets bought this way never gets finished, with around 2% of cosmetic items are thrown out from overproduction alone. A rate higher than fashion, pharmaceuticals, food, or the auto industry.

## `Slide 7 — THE TAKE`
 
**Headline:** Skin tint isn't over. It's just on its fourth name this decade.
**Supporting line:** Not everyone's buying at the same rate either. Some find their perfect match and stick with it for years. Others are five formats deep before they notice it's the same promise they already paid for, once under "skin tint," now under something else. The industry doesn't really care which one you are, as long as someone's buying.
