Buy Now, Owe Later. 🧵

For 20 years, fast fashion made clothes cheaper. That era ended around 2020, and now apparel prices are at their highest point in 26 years — climbing faster than wages can keep up.

How are people covering the gap? Increasingly, with debt. But BNPL isn't a neutral tool. Among the lowest-income users, 40% paid a bill late in 2024, more than 3x the rate of the highest-income users.

Fast fashion made clothes cheaper. Only some of us are paying for it later.
