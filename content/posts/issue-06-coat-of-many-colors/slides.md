## 01/08 — THE ITEM.

**Headline:** Function was never really the *question*.
**Body:**A basic kids' puffer jacket, the kind that shows up in every budget retailer's winter lineup. 

A coat from a brand that's been banned outright at multiple schools. Both zip up. Both keep a kid warm on the walk to school. Neither one is the "wrong" choice for staying warm.

---

## 02/08 — THE STAT.

**Headline:** A school banned **certain** coats.
**Body:** Not for warmth. Not for dress code. 

Woodchurch High School banned coats costing up to US $1,280, specifically so kids who couldn't afford them wouldn't feel singled out.

---

## 03/08 — THE PATTERN.

**Headline:** 91% could have told you exactly that *decades* ago.
**Body:** A 1996 study found brand names mattered so much to kids they threatened to undermine school uniform policy entirely. 

91% of students said brand names were a major reason for what they wore.

Kids realize fast which brands count and which ones don't, long before anyone explains why. Nobody teaches a seven-year-old what a status symbol is. They just learn which coat gets you left alone and which one doesn't. 

---

## 04/08 — THE BACKDROP.

*[6.1 - Apparel CPI vs, Wages]*

**Headline:** Prices didn't rise, Wages *outran* them.
**Body:** Clothes, on average, have gotten cheaper. A lot cheaper, relative to how much more people earn now. 

Wages climbed to roughly 13x the base year. Apparel costs barely tripled. That's not the twist you'd expect walking into a piece about affording clothes.

Citation: FRED (CPIAPPSL, AHETPI).

---

## 05/08 — THE MATH.

*[6.2 char — coat price gap]*

**Headline:** Fifty-seven times the price.
Zero times the difference in warmth.
**Body:** A basic kids' jacket: $14
 A coat that buys social safety: $791

Same job. 57x the price. 

Fast fashion made the basic garment cheap, and it did. But the basic garment was never the one a kid actually needed. The coat that keeps you from getting singled out didn't get cheaper. It got more expensive than ever.
Citation: Primark, Canada Goose, 2026.

---

## 06/08 — THE TAKE.

**Headline:** Cheap is a myth. 
Cruel is a *discount code*.
**Body:** Clothes have never been cheaper. The kind that keep you from getting made fun of still aren't. Somewhere along the way, the industry solved for price and left the actual problem sitting there untouched. 

Warmth was never on trial here. They get bullied because of what the coat says before anyone even talks to them. That part never went on sale.
